Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Spenso app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Deep saha (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as an Ad Supported service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. Below is a comprehensive list of all data collected by each service used in the Application:

The Application also collects general usage information including:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address)
  • The pages of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages
  • The time spent on the Application
  • The operating system you use on your mobile device

The Application does not gather precise information about the location of your mobile device.

The Application collects your device's location, which helps the Service Provider determine your approximate geographical location and make use of in below ways:

  • Geolocation Services: The Service Provider utilizes location data to provide features such as personalized content, relevant recommendations, and location-based services.
  • Analytics and Improvements: Aggregated and anonymized location data helps the Service Provider to analyze user behavior, identify trends, and improve the overall performance and functionality of the Application.
  • Third-Party Services: Periodically, the Service Provider may transmit anonymized location data to external services. These services assist them in enhancing the Application and optimizing their offerings.

Facebook SDK Data Collection

The Application uses the Facebook SDK, which may collect device identifiers including the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iOS devices. This data is used for usage analytics and to measure purchase events. The Facebook SDK helps us understand how users interact with the Application and may be used for advertising purposes. You can limit ad tracking in your device settings.

PostHog Analytics Data Collection

PostHog collects screen views, user behavior patterns, and device information (such as device type and operating system) to help us improve the Application experience. This analytics data is used to understand feature usage, identify issues, and make informed product decisions.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Data Collection

Google AdMob collects device identifiers and usage data to deliver personalized advertisements and measure ad performance. This may include information about the ads you view, interactions with ads, and general app usage patterns for ad targeting purposes.

The Service Provider may use the information you provided to contact you from time to time to provide you with important information, required notices and marketing promotions.

For a better experience, while using the Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to email, full name, and profile picture. The information that the Service Provider request will be retained by them and used as described in this privacy policy.

The Service Provider will retain User Provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter (typically up to 30 days after account deletion for backup and legal compliance purposes). How to Request Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. You can do this in two ways:

Option 1: In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open the Spenso application on your phone.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. After logging in, you will be redirected to the home screen.
  4. Tap on More in the bottom bar.
  5. The Settings screen will open—scroll to the bottom.
  6. Tap on Delete Your Account to proceed.

Option 2: Request via Form

You may also submit an account deletion request through our official form. Fill out the form to initiate the deletion process:

Delete Account Request Form

If you need assistance with the deletion process or have any questions, contact us at sahadeep125@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe (typically within 30 days).
